---
title: Cost Audit
description: We show you what's actually flowing through your infrastructure — including runaway automated and AI traffic — and catch cost and load anomalies before they run for months.
---

A considerable amount of expenditure is concealed within traffic that no one is observing: an integration retrying indefinitely, a background process that has grown by an order of magnitude, an automated or AI-driven routine invoking a metered service far more often than intended. Such patterns do not announce themselves. They continue — at times for months — until a bill or a load figure eventually compels attention. The recurring deficiency is not a discrete failure but the absence of anyone observing the meter.

Because this service occupies the point through which traffic passes, it can render visible what is actually moving through a system and identify the anomalies — the expenditure or load that has risen without cause — while they are still recent rather than long established. The result is visibility where there had been opacity, and early detection of the traffic that is quietly accruing cost.

The service applies where costs are rising without evident explanation, or where automation or AI has been introduced into production and its actual behavior under real conditions is unknown.

Its scope is that of observation and control at the level of traffic. It renders visible what is flowing and identifies what is anomalous from that position. It is not a comprehensive financial-operations platform and is not represented as one; what it provides is the visibility and the early detection that were absent.
