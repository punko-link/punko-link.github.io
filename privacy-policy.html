Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, and protected when you visit or use the website punkolink.com (the "Website").

1. Data Controller

The Website is operated by an individual (a natural person), not a registered company:

Glab Strizhkov
Email: support@punkolink.com

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR"), Glab Strizhkov is the data controller responsible for your personal data.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide to us

When you submit the "Contact Sales" form on the Website, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your message

2.2 Data collected automatically

When you browse the Website, we collect certain data automatically through Google Analytics (see Section 6), including:

  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Device and browser type
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and other usage statistics
  • Cookie identifiers

3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Purpose Data Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Responding to your inquiry submitted via the Contact Sales form Email, phone number, message content Art. 6(1)(b) — steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract; and/or Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries
Website analytics and improvement Usage data, cookie identifiers, IP address Art. 6(1)(a) — your consent (given via the cookie banner)
Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims; compliance with legal obligations Any of the above, as necessary Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 6(1)(f)

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you.

4. How Your Data Is Transmitted and Who Receives It

We share your personal data only with the following recipients:

4.1 Web3Forms (form processing service)

Data submitted through the Contact Sales form is transmitted to our mail server (punkolink.com) via Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form backend service. Web3Forms acts as a data processor: it relays your form submission to our email and does not use your data for its own purposes. For more information, see the Web3Forms privacy policy at https://web3forms.com/privacy.

4.2 Google LLC (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC (USA) / Google Ireland Limited (EU). Google processes usage data on our behalf as described in Section 6.

4.3 Other disclosures

We may disclose personal data if required by law, court order, or a lawful request by public authorities, or where necessary to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (in particular Google LLC and, depending on infrastructure, Web3Forms) may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, such transfers are made on the basis of:

  • an adequacy decision of the European Commission (e.g., the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified), and/or
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, together with supplementary measures where required.

You may contact us at support@punkolink.com to obtain more information about the safeguards applied.

6. Cookies and Google Analytics

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies.

Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the Website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as your IP address, device information, and browsing behaviour on the Website. Where technically available, IP anonymization is enabled.

Analytics cookies are set only with your consent, which you can give or refuse via the cookie consent banner when you first visit the Website. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your cookie preferences or deleting cookies in your browser settings.

You can additionally opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the browser add-on available at: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

More information about how Google processes data is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Retention

  • Contact form submissions (email, phone number, message): retained for as long as necessary to handle your inquiry and any follow-up communication, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you, unless a longer retention period is required to comply with legal obligations or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Analytics data: retained according to the retention settings configured in Google Analytics (up to 14 months), after which it is deleted or aggregated.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access, including the use of encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections for data transmission. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you are located in the EEA or the UK, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — to obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and a copy of it;
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20);
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — to object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) — with a supervisory authority in the EU/EEA member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@punkolink.com. We will respond within one month, as required by the GDPR. We may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

10. Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@punkolink.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Material changes will be communicated through a notice on the Website where appropriate.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, please contact:

Glab Strizhkov
Email: support@punkolink.com
