Pricing

What you buy

Punkolink runs on credits. Credits are priced at $1 each and sold in packs of 5 ($5); you can buy any multiple of a pack. On signup you receive 20 free credits to start — no card required.

Mechanics

How credits work

  • A credit is a unit of account — prepayment for Punkolink services. It is not a payment instrument, not e-money, and not a stored balance of funds.
  • A credit corresponds to $1 for pricing purposes only.
  • Credits are spent as you use services — containers, databases, and other resources draw down your credit balance as they run.
  • Credits cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, transferred, or redeemed for money.
Cloud pricing

How the cloud is priced

Every service — our own and the open-source ones — runs on DRP (Docker Run Platform), our system for running any container. DRP is also available on its own. A DRP instance, or any service running on it, is billed by the minute in credits and costs approximately 5 credits per month per running instance. The exact per-minute rates are set out in our Pricing Policy and published in the console.
