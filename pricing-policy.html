Pricing Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Scope

This Pricing Policy governs the pricing and use of credits for Punkolink services. It forms part of, and is subject to, the Punkolink Terms of Use.

2. Credits

2.1. A credit is a unit of account representing prepayment for Punkolink services.

2.2. A credit is not a payment instrument, not electronic money, and not a stored balance of funds.

2.3. A credit corresponds to USD 1.00 for pricing purposes only.

3. Purchase

3.1. Credits are sold in packs of five (5) credits at a price of USD 5.00 per pack.

3.2. Credits may be purchased in any whole multiple of one pack.

3.3. Purchases are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, acting as Merchant of Record. The applicable taxes are determined and charged by the Merchant of Record.

3.4. On account registration, Punkolink grants 20 credits at no charge. Granted credits are subject to this Policy.

4. Use of credits

4.1. Credits are consumed as Punkolink services are used. Consumption is measured against the resources used and deducted from the credit balance.

4.2. Consumption rates for each service are as published in the Punkolink console.

4.3. Credits are applied to Punkolink services only.

5. Restrictions

5.1. Credits cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, or exchanged for money.

5.2. Credits cannot be transferred between accounts.

5.3. Credits cannot be redeemed for any consideration other than Punkolink services.

6. Refunds

Refunds are governed by the Punkolink Refund Policy.

7. Engineering, consulting, and support services

Engineering, consulting, and support services are not sold as credits and are not purchased through this pricing. Such services are scoped and priced per engagement under separate agreement.

8. Changes

Punkolink may amend this Policy. The version in force is the one published at the time of purchase or use.
